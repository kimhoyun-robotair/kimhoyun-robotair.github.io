MarsLab documentation captures (19 November 2026)
  /images/marslab/captures/ and /videos/marslab/captures/
  Acquired by running the local MarsLab runtime in NVIDIA Isaac Sim 5.1.0.
  Rover models: Perseverance, Curiosity, Opportunity, and Spirit.
  Images are real simulator renders; depth and LiDAR plots use acquired arrays.
  Observation-camera orbit motion, controlled lighting changes, and one real
  controller-driven traversal are identified in the page captions.
  No MARE media or AI-generated imagery is included.
  Exact capture settings and output hashes: marslab/captures/provenance.json.
  Underlying MarsLab asset credits include:
    Rover visual sources: NASA/JPL-Caltech; Perseverance modeling by Zareh Gorjian.
    HiRISE terrain: NASA/JPL-Caltech/University of Arizona.
    Mars Rocks by Ivan Vakulko (milos4), CC BY 4.0:
      https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/mars-rocks-9f5c946255a24f1cb630ea95dceea587
      https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
    These source assets were rendered as part of the MarsLab scenes.
  See the MarsLab repository's asset and model source documentation for details.
