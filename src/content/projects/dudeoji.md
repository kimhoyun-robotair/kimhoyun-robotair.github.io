## Overview

**Dudeoji** was my undergraduate scale-car autonomous driving project entered in **AUTO RACE 2025**.

The [official competition announcement](https://cms.kookmin.ac.kr/fvedu/community/notice.do?articleNo=5929882&mode=view) from Kookmin University’s Future Automotive Program lists the final round on **November 28, 2025, at BEXCO in Busan**.

## Simulation and development

The project repository documents a simulation workflow using **ROS 2 Humble** and **Gazebo Harmonic**, together with localization components for camera observations, IMU data, and fused odometry.
