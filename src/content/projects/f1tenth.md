## Overview

In 2025, I was responsible for software development with **RCV Formula** as we prepared for **F1TENTH**, the predecessor to today's [RoboRacer](https://roboracer.ai/) competition. Teams competed in autonomous racing using a prescribed vehicle platform.

The competition rules prohibited **3D LiDAR**, so I developed **SLAM and state estimation** using **2D LiDAR, wheel odometry, and IMU measurements**. To prepare for racing, I also developed a custom **Gazebo** simulator and used it to validate the algorithms.

## Team and code

I am a **former team member** and am no longer affiliated with RCV Formula.

The simulation code is available in the [`Humble` branch of robotics-sim-tutorial-kr](https://github.com/kimhoyun-robotair/robotics-sim-tutorial-kr/tree/Humble).
